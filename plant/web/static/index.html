Schedule

How often the full sense → capture → describe → reason → act pipeline runs.

Every hour (60) is the default. Shorter intervals mean more photos and more AI calls.

Plant profile

Who the AI is looking after. The care notes are injected into every reasoning prompt, so this is where you shape its preferences.

Keep it factual: light preference, watering frequency, temperature range, overwatering sensitivity.

Safety limits

Guardrails that protect the plant if the AI gets it wrong. The AI sees these values and reasons within them; they don't dictate when to water. To shape the AI's preferences, edit the care notes in Plant.
